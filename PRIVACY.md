# HopDrop privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026. Applies to the HopDrop apps for Windows (installer, portable and Microsoft Store versions) and Android.

**Short version:** HopDrop collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no cloud. Your files go only to devices you paired yourself, directly over your own local network.

## What stays on your devices

- Files you send or receive. They travel straight between your devices over your local network (Wi-Fi, hotspot or Ethernet), encrypted, and are saved in the receive folder you choose. HopDrop never uploads them anywhere.
- A device identity (a key pair created on first start, kept in Windows' or Android's protected key storage), your paired devices, your settings and the Activity list of past transfers. You can delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

## What other devices can see

- Devices on the same local network can see your device's name and that it runs HopDrop. Settings → "Who can see this device's name" → "Only my paired devices" hides the name from everyone else.
- Only devices you paired (by QR code or by confirming the same 6-digit number on both screens) can send you files or receive files from you.

## Internet use

- Transfers never use the internet or mobile data.
- The Windows installer version and the Android app check GitHub Releases once a day for updates (this sends only a normal web request, like opening a web page, and nothing about you or your files). On Android you can turn it off in Settings → Check for updates; a new version is downloaded only when you tap Update. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store. The portable Windows app doesn't check for updates.

## AI agents (Windows, off by default)

If you turn on Settings → AI agents, AI assistants running on your own PC can list your paired devices, send files to them and see which files arrived. This happens only on your PC; HopDrop sends nothing to the assistant's company. What an assistant does with what it reads is covered by that assistant's own privacy policy.

## Children

HopDrop collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
